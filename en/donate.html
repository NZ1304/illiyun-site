Support Illiyun

Illiyun is free, without ads and without paid features, and will stay that way. Donations keep it running and improving: servers, sign-in emails, app store publication, work on new features.

Online donations will open on this page soon. Until then, to offer your help, write to contact@illiyun.app.

Telling others about the app, or reporting a mistake in the text, is valuable help too.
