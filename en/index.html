Illiyun

Read the Quran page by page, as it is printed, then understand each verse and each word.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Read

The Madinah mushaf in Hafs, and the King Fahd Complex mushaf in Warsh, with the exact layout of every page. Every word responds to touch: a tap marks it, a double tap opens its definition, a long press marks the whole verse.

For tired eyes: large text in Warsh, thicker strokes, high contrast, dark mode, and tajweed colours in Hafs.

Understand

For each verse, a tafsir of your choice (al-Muyassar, as-Saʿdī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Mukhtaṣar in English and French) and English and French translations. The verse is shown in its place in the mushaf, and each word leads to its word-by-word translation, its root and the entry in al-Mufradāt or Lane.

Keep your place

Resume where you stopped, bookmark pages and verses, get a daily reminder. An account is optional: it only brings your settings and bookmarks to another phone.

Free, without ads

Illiyun has no ads, no paid features and no audience tracking. If you would like to help with its costs, the Support page explains how.
