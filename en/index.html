Surah al-Muṭaffifīn, verses 18 and 19

Illiyun

Read the Quran page by page, as it is printed, then understand each verse and each word.

Coming soon to Google Play.

Read

The Madinah mushaf in Hafs and the King Fahd Complex mushaf in Warsh, with the exact layout of every page. Every word responds to touch: a tap marks it, a double tap opens its definition, a long press marks the whole verse.

For tired eyes: pinch zoom or a magnifier button, large text in Warsh, high contrast, dark mode, tajweed colours in Hafs, and a colour-blind mode.

Listen

The great reciters, verse by verse or continuously, with the page following along. Repeat a passage to learn it, adjust the speed, set a sleep timer, and download to listen offline.

Understand

For each verse, tafsirs and translations in several languages (Arabic, French, English, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, German). Each word leads to its word-by-word translation, its root and the Illiyun Lexicon, with the other words of the same root.

Memorise

Mark each word known or difficult, test yourself with the text hidden, follow your progress verse by verse and come back to your difficulties.

Keep your place

Resume where you stopped, colour bookmarks, a daily reminder, and share a verse as an image. An account is optional: it only brings your settings and progress to another phone.

Free, without ads

Illiyun has no ads, no paid features and no audience tracking. If you would like to help with its costs, the Support page explains how.
